# Car Performance Meter - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Car Performance Meter ("the app", package `com.agappsproduction.carperformance`) is a car performance measuring app for Android, published by AG Apps Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and how you control it.

## Summary

Car Performance Meter has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your measurements, GPS tracks and settings are stored only on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone automatically is an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) sent to a weather service, and the map area you view, sent to a map-tile server. Both can be avoided (see below).

## What the app uses and stores on your device

**Location (precise GPS).** Used while a measurement is running to determine speed and distance, and to draw the route of each run. GPS data is processed on the device and saved with your runs on the device. The app uses location only while you are measuring (a foreground service with a visible notification); it does not access location in the background when no measurement is active.

**Motion sensors** (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer). Used to measure acceleration, power and torque. Processed on the device. If you enable *Record raw sensor data* in Settings, the raw readings are saved as files on the device for diagnostics; they are never uploaded.

**Vehicle data from Android Auto.** On Android Auto, the *Car data* page can show information the car shares with Android Auto (vehicle make and model, speed, fuel or battery level, range, odometer and the car's own sensors), after you grant the corresponding permissions. It is only displayed; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

**Vehicle details** you enter (mass, tyre size, drag figures). Stored on the device and used only for calculations.

## Data sent off the device

**Weather (Open-Meteo).** When *Fetch weather* is enabled in Settings, at the start of a run the app sends your position rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) over an encrypted connection, to obtain air temperature, humidity and pressure for power correction. No identifier of you or your device is included. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy apply to that request. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

**Map tiles (OpenStreetMap).** When you open a run's route map, the app downloads map images for the area shown from the OpenStreetMap tile servers. As with any web request, those servers see your IP address and the requested map area. The [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) applies. Tiles are cached on the device.

**Actions you start yourself.** If you tap *Open in Google Maps* or *Share* on a result, the route coordinates or the result text are passed to the app you choose; that app's privacy policy then applies.

## What we do not do

We do not collect, store or receive any of your data on our own servers, do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising, and do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data (results and settings) in the backup it makes to your Google account. That backup is made by Android and stored by Google under your Google account, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.

## Children

The app is intended for adult drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

## Safety

Measure only where it is legal and safe, such as a closed track. Do not operate the phone while driving.

## Deleting your data

All data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual results in the app, delete raw recordings in Settings, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
