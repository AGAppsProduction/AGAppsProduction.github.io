# Workout Timer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Android app "Workout Timer" (package `com.agappsproduction.workouttimer`), published by [DEVELOPER NAME].

## Summary

Workout Timer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no internet permission.

## Data stored on your device

The timers you create (names, intervals, colours and the messages you type) and your app settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If you have Android backup switched on for your device, Android may include this app data in your own device backup. That backup is managed by your device and your backup provider, not by the developer of this app.

## Spoken messages

To read your interval messages aloud, the app passes their text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. How that engine handles text is governed by the engine's own provider.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** shows the running workout with pause and stop buttons.
- **Foreground service and wake lock:** keep the timer, voice and beeps running while the screen is off.
- **Vibration:** optional vibration when an interval changes.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
