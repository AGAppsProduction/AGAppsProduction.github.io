# Workout Timer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Workout Timer ("the app", package `com.agappsproduction.workouttimer`) is an interval timer app for Android, published by AG Apps Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

Workout Timer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission.

## What the app stores on your device

The timers you create (names, intervals, colours and the messages you type) and your app settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see them or access them.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app data in your own device backup. That backup is managed by your device and your backup provider, not by us, and we have no access to it.

## Spoken messages (text-to-speech)

To read your interval messages aloud, the app passes their text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. How that engine handles text is governed by the engine's own provider.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** shows the running workout with pause and stop buttons.
- **Foreground service and wake lock:** keep the timer, voice and beeps running while the screen is off.
- **Vibration:** optional vibration when an interval changes.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

All data is on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes your timers and settings.
