# Easy vocabulary - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Easy vocabulary ("the app") is a vocabulary learning app for Android, published by AG Apps Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

Easy vocabulary does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

## What the app stores, and where

To remember your learning, the app keeps one file on your device. It contains:

- the language you know and the language you are learning,
- which words you have practised and when they are due for review,
- how many minutes you studied each day,
- your settings, such as whether pronunciation is turned on.

This file never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it or access it. It contains no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this progress file in the backup it makes to your Google account, so your progress is restored on a new phone. That backup is made by Android and stored by Google under your Google account, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.

## Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

The app pronounces words using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The app only passes the word to be spoken to that engine. The engine is part of your device and is provided by its maker (for example Google or Samsung), under that provider's own privacy terms.

## Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

## Children

The app is suitable for children and is made with young learners in mind. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links to social media.

## Deleting your data

All data is on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Easy vocabulary > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if an optional online feature is added, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
