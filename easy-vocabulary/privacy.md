# Easy vocabulary - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Easy vocabulary ("the app", package `com.agappsproduction.easyvocabulary`) is a vocabulary learning app for Android, published by AG Apps Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

Easy vocabulary has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across apps. Your learning progress stays on your device. From version 0.4.0 the app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example how many words were learned in a lesson and how long it took) so that we can improve the lessons. The statistics contain no name, no contact details and nothing you type, and you can switch them off in the app's settings. Versions before 0.4.0 do not use the internet at all.

## What the app stores on your device

To remember your learning, the app keeps one file on your device. It contains:

- the language you know and the language you are learning,
- which words you have practised and when they are due for review,
- how many minutes you studied each day,
- your settings, such as whether pronunciation is turned on.

This file is not sent to us. We cannot see it or access it. It contains no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

## Anonymous usage statistics

From version 0.4.0, the app makes one short summary for each lesson and sends it to our server when the phone is online. The summary contains only counts:

- the topic of the lesson and the kind of lesson (learning, test or review),
- how many words the lesson had and how many of them were learned,
- how many times words were entered or picked, and how many of those were right,
- whether the lesson was finished, and how many seconds were spent learning,
- which extra languages were switched on under "Also show translations in", and how many times the hint with those translations was opened.

The app does not record individual answers, which words were right or wrong, or anything that is typed. Summaries are sent after a lesson or when the app is closed; a lesson that was interrupted is sent the next time the app is opened. If no lesson was started, nothing is sent.

With each summary the app sends the time the lesson ended, the app version, the Android version number, the two languages chosen in the app, and a random identifier.

About the random identifier: it is a random number created by the app when it is first used. It lets us count how many installations use the app and tell that two lessons came from the same installation. It is not your name or account, it is not the Android advertising ID, and it is not derived from your phone. It is not included in device backups, so it is not copied to a new phone, and it is deleted when you switch statistics off, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

The statistics never contain: your name, email address, phone number, account, location, contacts, photos, the advertising ID, the phone's model or serial numbers, or any text you type.

**Why we collect them.** Only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, for example to find topics that are too hard or lessons that are too long. We do not use them for advertising or profiling and we do not try to identify anyone.

**Where they go.** The statistics are sent over an encrypted connection to a server we operate on Google Cloud in Finland (European Union) and are stored there in Google BigQuery. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. We do not sell the statistics or share them with anyone else. To deliver the upload, the phone's IP address is necessarily visible to the network and to Google's servers while the request is handled, as with any internet connection; we do not store it with the statistics and we have switched off the server's request logs.

**App integrity check.** To make sure uploads come from the genuine app, the app uses Google's Firebase App Check with the Play Integrity service. These Google services check on the device that the app is the real one installed from Google Play and give the app a short-lived token. This check is performed by Google under Google's privacy policy ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

**How long they are kept.** Statistics are deleted automatically 400 days after we receive them.

**Switching them off.** Open Settings in the app and turn off "Share anonymous statistics". The app then stops recording and sending statistics, deletes the events that were waiting to be sent, and deletes the random identifier. The app works exactly the same with statistics switched off, and it works without an internet connection.

**Your rights.** Because the statistics are not linked to your identity, we normally cannot find the records of a particular person. If you have questions or a request about your data, contact us at the address below. If you are in the European Economic Area you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the progress file in the backup it makes to your Google account, so your progress is restored on a new phone. That backup is made by Android and stored by Google under your Google account, not by us, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time. The random identifier used for statistics is not part of the backup.

## Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

The app pronounces words using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The app only passes the word to be spoken to that engine. The engine is part of your device and is provided by its maker (for example Google or Samsung), under that provider's own privacy terms.

## Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. From version 0.4.0 it requests internet access, which is used only to send the anonymous statistics described above. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

## Children

The app is suitable for children and is made with young learners in mind. It does not ask for or collect names, contact details, photos, voice recordings or location from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage statistics described above are used only to support and improve the app itself, never for advertising or to build a profile of a child. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links to social media. A parent or guardian can switch the statistics off in the app's settings at any time, and can contact us with any question at the address below.

## Deleting your data

Your learning progress is on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Easy vocabulary > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. Either also deletes the random identifier. Statistics already sent are not linked to you and are deleted automatically after 400 days.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released.
