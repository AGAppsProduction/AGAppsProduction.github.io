Easy vocabulary

Learn the 1000 most useful words in 7 languages. Listen, play and spell, one small topic at a time.

  • 1
    Short, friendly lessons. 57 topics of 15 to 20 words, from greetings and family to food, school and travel.
  • 2
    Listen, play, spell. Hear every word, pick the right translation, then spell it on a simple keyboard where each letter always sits on the same key.
  • 3
    Words that stick. Known words come back for quick reviews just before you would forget them.
  • 4
    Any language pair. Learn Spanish from English, Finnish from Russian, German from Swedish, and more.
